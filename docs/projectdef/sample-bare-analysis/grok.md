## Weaknesses (this is what is leaking rating)

**1. Unprotected pieces.** Queen on e3/d4, bishop on h6, knight on f3 while the queen leaves. Before *every* move, the question is: “If I let go of this piece, can they take it for free?”

**2. The English has no second gear.** `c4 + g3 + Bg2` is a starting formation, not a plan. You do not have a default answer to:
- Reversed Sicilian (`...e5`)
- Symmetrical (`...c5`)
- King’s Indian setup (`...g6`)
- `...e6` / French structures

So you invent on the board: random `h3`, `a3`, `b3`, `Qb3-Qd1`, `Nh4`, pawn grabs.

**3. You give up the fianchetto bishop cheaply.** Many White losses start with `...Bh3` or `...Bg4xf3` and then your king is a dark-square patient.

**4. Calculation depth is often 1 ply.** You see your idea. You do not see their reply. `Qxe3` without looking at `Bd4`. `Qd4` without looking at `Nxd4`. `Bh6` without looking at `Bxh6`.

**5. Back rank and king hunts as Black.** yomamaismymama6789’s `Rd8#` is a textbook back-rank mate. Several Scandi games you castle into an attack and never challenge the knight on e5.

**6. Sidelines of your own repertoire.**  
- Advance Scandi `2.e5` (Stefi, 1755Ata): you play `...c5` and then get squeezed or mated on the kingside.  
- `3.c4 Qd8` (Kakovf): you got your king ripped after `...gxf6`.

**7. Volume without review.** Nine games a day means the same hang happens on Tuesday and again on Friday.

## What you should focus on *right now*

Not “learn 20 English lines.” Not “switch to the Najdorf.” Three things, in order.

### Focus 1 — A 3-second blunder check (highest Elo per hour)
Every move, before you click:

1. Is my queen / rook / bishop / knight hanging?
2. What did their last move threaten?
3. If I take this, can they take back *and* check me?

Do 15–20 **hanging-piece / capture puzzles** a day for two weeks. Your rating will move more from this than from any opening video.

### Focus 2 — Give White a plan, or change the opening
You have two honest options.

**Option A (keep 1.c4):** pick *one* system and repeat it.

- vs `1...e5`: play **`e3`, `Nge2`, `d4`**. You already scored with this. Do not play the slow `d3 + Qb3` game. When their bishop is on c5, hit the center.
- vs `1...c5`: play `Nf3`, `d4` or a Botvinnik setup (`e4/c4/d3` with `Nge2`) — but pick one and stop mixing.
- Never leave g2 undefended against `...Bh3` without a reason. If they go `...Bh3`, consider `Bh1` or meeting it only after `Re1`/`Kh2`.
- Stop the `Qb3` then `Qd1` waste.

**Option B (simpler, and I would lean this way at your rating):** switch White to the **London** or **Italian**. You already like systems. The London gives you a plan against everything (`Bf4`, `e3`, `Nf3`, `c3`, `Nbd2`) and you will stop leaking 4–5 games a week as White. Your Black is already carrying you. White only needs to be *average*.

### Focus 3 — Two Scandinavian patches, not a new Black opening
Keep `1...d5`. Add only this:

- vs **`2.e5`**: don’t autopilot `...c5` into a French Advance you don’t know. Learn one setup: `...c5`, `...Nc6`, `...Bf5` or `...Bg4`, and **do not play `...Bxf3` then `...O-O` into `Ng5`** the way you did vs Stefi (`Bxg6`, `Nxe6`, exchange gone).
- vs **`O-O-O` + `h4`**: play `...c6`, `...Nbd7`, `...Qc7` or get the queen off `a5` early. Meet `h4` with `...h6` or `...h5`, not `...g6` unless you have already traded the attacking bishop.
- vs **`3.c4`**: retreat **`Qd8` or `Qd6`**, don’t linger on d5, and don’t allow `d5` plus `Bxf6 gxf6` without knowing you can live with the wrecked king.
