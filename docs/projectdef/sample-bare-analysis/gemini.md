## Actionable Plan to Break to the Next Level

* **Implement a 3-Second "Blunder Check"**: Before finalizing forcing or central moves, explicitly scan for opponent knight jumps to $c3/e3/b3/d4$ and exposed diagonals connecting your Queen/King.

* **Proactively Blockade Passed Pawns**: Identify enemy passed pawns early in the middlegame or endgame transition and place a piece (ideally a Knight) directly in front of them before they gather momentum.
