# `/roadmap`, `/drills`, `/drills/[id]` — syllabus and practice

Training surfaces. Always in the rail. Analysis-plan players see the truth and a Training CTA —
we do not hide the product.

## `/roadmap`

Ordered path from this profile toward what a stable player at the next band already does. A
workbook table of contents, not a course catalog and not a cartoon trail.

### On the desk now

Empty syllabus plate. No canned “Beginner → Master” tree.

### Later

Steps from the product def only:

- Blunder-preventer
- Replay own mistakes
- Defend worse
- Convert advantage
- Make a plan (intermediate+)

Each step: skill, why (a profile sentence), evidence games, drill ids, done-when that is
behavioral (“you stop hanging the English bishop to …Bh3 in the next 15 White games”), not XP.
Current step gets the one gold rule. In-page `#s3`, no `/roadmap/[step]`.

Empty without a profile: “A roadmap without a profile is a generic tactics book. Wait for the
writeup.”

## `/drills`

Assigned queue from the roadmap (and from “Drill this” on a game). Not Puzzle Storm.

### On the desk now

Empty queue plate.

### Later

Group by roadmap step or type. Row: type, one-line stem from their game, source game, last
attempt. Filters: type, due / done. No drill until the roadmap names a leak.

## `/drills/[id]`

One position, one job, the student moves.

### On the desk now

Do not open a playable board. “This drill has no position yet. We will not put a textbook mate
in front of you and call it yours.”

### Later

Stem (why you). Board. On miss: the idea in words + the refutation arrow, not “−2.4.” On hit:
what to notice next time. Try again / See the game (`/games/[id]?ply=`) / Next drill / Back to
step. Types: replay the mistake ply; defend a worse position from their loss; convert an
advantage they dumped; what’s the plan; blunder-preventer (hangs / last-move threat / 3–4 move
combo).
