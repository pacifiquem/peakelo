# `/home` — coach desk

Default logged-in landing. Answers “what do I do in the next 20 minutes?”

## On the desk now

- One-sentence status of the coaching pass (honest: games imported, profile not written).
- Real imported games that have no writeup yet (names from `/games`, not invented lessons).
- Sync line: new games land about every 30 minutes.
- Links into Profile, Games, Roadmap, Drills.

## Later (engine pass + training)

1. Player headline — one sentence from the profile writeup.
2. Today’s work — a single CTA: continue a named drill, review a named game, or open a named
   roadmap step.
3. Waiting on you — at most three unreviewed games or unread writeups.
4. No source switcher, no W/L, no ACPL, no dual CTAs.

## Empty copy

- Games in, no profile: “Your games are in. We have not written who you are yet — that takes a
  full engine pass, not a glance at the scoresheet.”
- No games: “Nothing to coach yet. Play a rated blitz, rapid, or bullet game on the account you
  linked.”
- Analysis job running (when that job exists): “Reading every move of the last N games. This page
  stays empty until that pass finishes.”
